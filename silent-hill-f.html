Bug reports

SH-301 · Major · Open · 80% repro

Flashlight beam clips through the hospital examination room wall

The flashlight volume illuminates props and geometry behind a solid wall. Steps: Enter the hospital examination wing; stand beside the examination room door; aim across the wall seam. Observed results: The beam leaks through the wall and reveals objects in the next room. Expected results: The flashlight beam is occluded by the wall.

SH-302 · Major · In Progress · 60% repro

Radio static continues after the nearby enemy leaves the search area

The radio warning state remains active after the enemy moves beyond the encounter radius. Steps: Enter the apartment hallway; trigger the warning; move two rooms away; wait for the enemy to leave. Observed results: Static continues until a checkpoint reload. Expected results: Static stops when no hostile is inside the detection radius.

SH-303 · Minor · Review · 100% repro

Puzzle note text overlaps the inventory panel at 16:10

The translated puzzle note extends underneath the inventory panel on a narrow display configuration. Steps: Set 16:10; collect the translated note; open the inventory. Observed results: Final lines are partially hidden. Expected results: All puzzle note text remains readable.
