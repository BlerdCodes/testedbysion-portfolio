BUG-2457 Open
Summary
[Bug] Character model flickers during cutscene transitions
Description
The character model briefly flickers, clips through the environment, and snaps back into position when entering a zone transition during gameplay. This issue is most noticeable when moving between open-world map sections and during scripted scene transitions.
Repro rate
8/10 - 80%
Severity
major
Environment
gameplay
Steps to reproduce
1. Launch the game and load a saved campaign file. 2. Travel to a map transition area or loading gate. 3. Walk toward the trigger while the scene begins to change. 4. Observe the player mesh flicker and briefly clip into the terrain.
Actual behavior
The same character model temporarily disappears, flickers, and reappears offset or intersecting the ground during loading transitions. The bug interrupts the player experience and can make movement feel inconsistent.
Expected behavior
The character should transition cleanly into the next area without clipping, disappearing, or losing alignment with the environment. The animation and mesh should remain stable throughout the transition.
Build
1.7.4
Labels
visual, animation, gameplay
Media
Screenshot / video attachment
Additional notes
Occurs more frequently after changing outfits in the character creator. Temporary workaround: lower shadow quality. The issue still appears on mid-range hardware, though it is more visible on high-detail settings.
