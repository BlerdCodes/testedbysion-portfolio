Bug reports

NBA-201 · Critical · Open · 90% repro

MyCAREER badge progress does not update after a completed NBA game

Post-game badge progress remains unchanged after the tracked action is performed. Steps: Load a MyCAREER save; equip a finishing badge; perform the action; finish the game. Observed results: The badge value remains at its pre-game amount. Expected results: Completed-game actions update the post-game progression screen.

NBA-202 · Major · In Progress · 70% repro

Shot meter setting resets after returning from the practice facility

The selected shot meter style is not retained after leaving practice and loading back into the City. Steps: Set a custom shot meter; enter practice; return to the City; open controller settings. Observed results: The shot meter reverts to the default style. Expected results: The selected style persists across mode transitions.

NBA-203 · Minor · Resolved · 50% repro

Crowd audio loops after a timeout during online play

A short crowd reaction repeats continuously after the timeout overlay closes. Steps: Start an online match; call a timeout during a scoring run; resume play. Observed results: The same clip loops until the next whistle. Expected results: The arena mix returns after the timeout.
